How the analysis works

01

The control score

TCI combines territory (30%), progressive passing (25%), pass completion (20%), and shots (25%). It is an experimental score, not an established measure of team quality.

02

When tracking is needed

Player positions let us estimate space control and team shape. If your data only contains events, these charts stay blank.

03

What gets checked

The checker compares findings with the calculated numbers and flags small samples. A person still needs to judge whether the football interpretation makes sense.
