Optional: learn TCI weights from future xG

TCI combines several statistics into a control score. This tool learns its weights from Training events to predict the team's share of xG in the following fifteen playing minutes. xG means expected goals, a provider's estimate of chance quality. Every shot or goal needs an xg label; ordinary analysis does not require xG.

The model download saves the weights for reuse. Validation remains unseen during development. This weight-training target is separate from the dashboard's untrained next-phase trend estimate.

The included catalogue and Real Madrid vs Barcelona events have no xG labels, so original weights are used until you supply training data.

HOW IT WORKS

Understand the dashboard

01

Observe the match

Event records describe actions such as passes and shots. Optional tracking records player locations. Match catalogues alone contain fixtures and results.

02

Check the interpretation

Statistics are calculated in code. Analyst findings cite those statistics. Numeric checks compare values and samples; AI review examines reasoning and contradictions.

03

Plan what to try

The team outlook carries recent patterns into a conditional next-phase estimate. Suggested match actions, practice ideas and monitoring priorities are tied to the selected team's evidence.
