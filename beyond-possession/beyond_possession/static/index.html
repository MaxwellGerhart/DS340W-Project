HOW IT WORKS

How the analysis works

01

The control score

TCI starts with territory (30%), progressive passing (25%), completion (20%) and shots (25%). You can instead fit nonnegative weights on Training matches to predict future xG share, then compare them on disjoint Test matches. Neither version is a validated measure of control.

02

When tracking is needed

Player positions let us estimate space control and team shape. If your data only contains events, these charts stay blank.

03

What gets checked

Free-written findings carry citations for every number. Failed drafts get one correction attempt. The hybrid critic checks numbers first, then a separate AI call reviews tactics and contradictions. AI review can still be wrong; the original numeric critic remains available.
